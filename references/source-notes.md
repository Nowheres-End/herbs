# Sources and editorial notes

- Herb list, blend membership, historical descriptions, and public herb entries: [Nowhere’s End herb library](https://nowhere.terpedia.com/data/herbs.json), accessed 2026-11-24. Individual source pages use the same current library at `https://nowhere.terpedia.com/herbs/{slug}.html`.
- Terpedia organism QR destinations use exact organism records where those routes resolved. For Hibiscus sabdariffa, Rosa spp., Gentiana lutea, Rosa canina, and Calendula officinalis, an exact route was not available during checking; those QR codes open the Terpedia organism directory (`https://terpedia.com/organism`) instead of a broken page.
- Blend label identity and image links:

- TTB records substantiate label names/art only. Herb-to-blend assignment is sourced to the public herb library.
- Each summary of medicinal purposes is framed as traditional/historical use. It is not a claim of clinical efficacy or advice to treat a condition.
- Botanical plates and cover artwork were generated for this project.
